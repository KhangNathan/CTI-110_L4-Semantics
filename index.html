Overview

Rainbow Six Siege is a first person shooter that focuses mainly on a 5v5 bomb planting/defusing gamemode. This game is owned by Ubisoft Montreal, and is technically Siege X, a sequel to the base siege game. This game focused a lot on audio cues, positioning & setups, and aiming, specifically recoil control & crosshair placement. There are unique operators for both the attacking and defending sides, with 1 ability and 1 utility item each.

Attacker operators have abilities that aid them in pushing onto a bomb site, with certain gadgets, such as grenades or breaches to compliment that. Defender operators have abilities to allow them to hold down sites and fight against advancing enemies, with utility to allow for slowing down pushes and shaping the field to their advantage. Another key aspect of this game is the ability to wallbang, with different walls in the game having different "breaking points" and guns having different destruction levels. This allows for the creation of headholes and rotations to make each round unique.

R6 Siege also has many interesting movement mechanics, which is quite different from other shooter games. One thing new players notice almost immediately is the inability to jump in the game, there is only mantling onto platforms. This is extremely different from games such as COD, Valorant, or Fortnite, where fast paced movement is key in winning gun fights. There is crouching and proning in the game, which is not as surprising, but allows for strategic positioning and silent movement to catch opponent's off guard. Finally, leaning left and right, usually around walls, is a key aspect in the gunfights as pixel angles and crosshair placement are large factors to attribute to if a player can win a gunfight or not.
